Hushborn YouTube Uploader is a desktop utility used by the Hushborn channel owner to upload original narrated stories and their cover artwork to YouTube.

What the tool does

Uploads a video and custom thumbnail, sets its title and description, and checks the destination channel and video status. Uploads made by this tool are private. Public release is a separate decision by the channel owner.

This website explains the utility. It does not accept uploads, perform Google sign-in, or collect Google credentials.

Why Google access is requested

Manage YouTube videos: upload the selected video and its thumbnail to the authorized channel.

View the YouTube account: confirm the channel identity and check the uploaded video's details, privacy, and processing status.

Google authorization takes place in the browser through Google's OAuth consent screen. The utility is for the owner's use; it is not a public upload service.

Control stays with the owner

Local files are selected explicitly. When remembered authorization is enabled, the refresh credential is kept in the owner's macOS Keychain. Access can be revoked in the Google account at any time.
